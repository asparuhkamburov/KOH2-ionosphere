KOH2 2025-001: GEOMETRY-FREE PHASE vs pyOASIS ROTI/DTEC
======================================================================

Scope
-----
Event window: 2025-01-01 16:50-17:10 UTC
Pre window:   2025-01-01 16:30-16:50 UTC
Post window:  2025-01-01 17:10-17:30 UTC
Strict geometry sensitivity: elevation >= 40 deg

Main result
-----------
The pyOASIS comparison provides PARTIAL, satellite-specific support for the
geometry-free event, but it does not independently confirm the full
cross-constellation event.

Interpretation
--------------
1. R04 provides the strongest local independent-like cross-check:
   * ROTI reaches 0.86942 near 17:06 UTC, the largest R04 ROTI value in the
     elevation>=40 deg daily subset.
   * |DTEC| reaches 4.93158 near 17:05:30 UTC, above the R04 daily p95 in
     the elevation>=40 deg subset.
   * The GF R04 enhancement occurs around 17:03-17:05 UTC, so the three
     measures overlap within the different sampling windows.

2. This R04 disturbance is broader than the exact 16:50-17:10 GF window.
   Elevated R04 DTEC continues after 17:10, including 5.00066 at 17:13 UTC.
   Therefore the data support a disturbed interval, not a sharp one-minute onset.

3. R21 does NOT provide the same confirmation during 16:50-17:10:
   its ROTI and |DTEC| remain below their own daily medians/p95 levels during
   the strongest GF enhancement. R21's larger ROTI/DTEC excursions occur later.

4. The pyOASIS GPS products available in the event interval contain G02 and
   G21, but not the strongest GF GPS candidates G01 and G10. G02/G21 ROTI/DTEC
   remain relatively quiet during 16:50-17:10. Therefore the pyOASIS products
   cannot independently verify the G01/G10 part of the cross-constellation GF
   cluster.

   relationship overall. Within-satellite rank correlations:

Conclusion
----------
The ROTI/DTEC comparison strengthens the interpretation that at least part of
the 16:50-17:10 geometry-free activity is ionospheric/disturbance-related,
especially for R04. It does NOT yet validate the entire multi-satellite GF
cluster as ionospheric scintillation.

The high-rate module should remain EXPERIMENTAL. The most defensible current
wording is "geometry-free phase-fluctuation proxy associated with a disturbed
ionospheric interval", not validated Phi60/scintillation.
