Independent geomagnetic context
-------------------------------
NOAA/SWPC planetary Kp for the 15-18 UTC synoptic interval was 8.0 (G4 range).

The archived real-time Dst series has:
  15 UTC: -220 nT
  16 UTC: -221 nT
  17 UTC: -198 nT
  18 UTC: -160 nT

Therefore the 16:50-17:10 UTC KOH2 cluster occurred immediately after the
storm-time Dst minimum, during the beginning of Dst recovery, while the
planetary Kp interval remained at 8.

NOAA reported that the G4 Severe alert threshold itself was reached at
17:41 UTC (12:41 ET), later within the same 15-18 UTC Kp interval.

A secondary solar-wind chronology based on DSCOVR/SWPC data reports a long
southward IMF Bz interval to about 16:45 UTC followed by a northward turn.
The KOH2 cluster begins approximately 10 minutes after that reported turn.
This timing is suggestive but is not treated as proof of causation.

Internal amplitude-side cross-check
-----------------------------------
The same-receiver S4_CNO_PROXY is not independent and is not calibrated ISMR S4.
Nevertheless, its 16:58 UTC minute is among the stronger daily minute-level
S4-proxy excursions. This is supporting internal context only.

Current interpretation
----------------------
The geometry-free event survives:
  * dual-frequency common-mode cancellation,
  * LLI/cycle-slip segmentation,
  * 120-s segment-edge QC,
  * SP3 geometry,
  * elevation >=40 deg,
  * and appears simultaneously on GPS and GLONASS links.

Its timing also overlaps the strongest geomagnetic-storm interval and follows
the Dst minimum closely.

However, this is still insufficient to claim validated ionospheric
scintillation. The next independent local/regional comparison should use
KOH2 ROTI/DTEC/TEC and, if available, external scintillation measurements.

The high-rate module should remain labelled EXPERIMENTAL.
